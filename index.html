02 — PocketCheck

Play it for real. See where you actually land.

Play the groove on your own kit while Tating listens through the microphone, then shows exactly where every hit landed against the beat. It measures the speaker-to-mic delay first, so the reading is honest — and it reports placement, not a grade. Sitting behind the beat is a feel. What matters is how tight the cloud is. Audio is analysed in memory and never recorded or sent anywhere.
